Notional is the face value of the contracts held, not money at risk and not borrowing. A futures book posts margin, so gross notional many times equity is ordinary for the asset class; it is reported here because it is the number a reader cannot otherwise get, not because it is a leverage ratio in the lending sense.

Attribution is gross of commission and shows where the P&L was earned, not what each market contributed after cost. Commission is a portfolio-level figure on the Daily P&L page.
