About the summary figures.

    Checking this yourself. Each executed row shows the open it filled at and the close it was decided on. Contract point values are on the Mapping page, so qty × (close − open) × pointsize × FX reproduces the position's move for the session.

    Realised P&L is the share of a position's accumulated profit that the trade on that row banked, not the position's move for the session. A trade that adds to a position realises nothing; one that halves it realises half of what the position had accrued; a close realises the rest. A winner you are still holding therefore shows zero, and every dollar the book has made is either realised on some row or still sitting in an open position.
